## Required scientific corrections

### R1 — P1: reverse-oriented community member bypasses a zero uptake cap

**Source:** `cmig/core/dfba_community.py:run_community_dfba`, the per-step `reaction.lower_bound = -uptake` assignment, and `_default_member_vmax`'s lower-bound-only fallback. MICOM retains the orientation of member↔pool reactions; it does not universally convert them into negative-uptake reactions.

**Actual MICOM/Gurobi reproduction:** two equal-biomass members, tracked glucose concentration 1, initial biomasses 0.1 each, `dt=t_end=0.1`, M0 glucose Vmax **0**, M1 Vmax **10**, closure on. Conventional M0 remains at **0.1**, growth/uptake **0**; changing only M0's input exchange to reverse-unit orientation produces M0 biomass **0.14950501**, growth/uptake **4.950501**. Both results say `completed`, `full_flux_at_every_step=true`, `interpretable=true`. The total environmental depletion happens to match, so a community-total balance check alone misses the invalid member kinetics.

Evidence: `probe_community_limits.py`, `community-limits.json`, `community-limits.log`. This is a measured optimum, not injected status. Escalated as `msg_c2fbd1363497` and accepted by the coordinator.

### R2 — P1: duplicate channels multiply one member's nutrient allowance

**Source:** the new list-valued `_member_exchange_map` plus the same per-step loop, which assigns the entire configured `uptake` to every duplicate reaction.

**Actual reproduction:** two conventional members, each initially 0.1, glucose 1, M0 Vmax **2**, M1 Vmax **10**. With one M0 glucose channel, M0 uptake is **1.98019802** and final biomass **0.11980198**. Adding a second valid glucose exchange without changing the configured member/nutrient Vmax gives uptake **3.96039604** and biomass **0.13960396**. M1's uptake changes from **9.90099010** to **7.92079208**, maintaining the environmental cap while allocating M0 twice its permitted uptake. Acceptance remains interpretable. The same evidence files contain both controls.

**Astra correction contract for R1/R2, requested by the coordinator:**

1. Derive every member-channel physical coordinate from verified topology. For member abundance `a_i>0`, pool coefficient `c_pool,r` and raw reaction flux `v_r`, use **`q_i,r=(c_pool,r/a_i)·v_r`**, positive secretion and negative uptake, in units per member biomass. Do not apply another abundance factor until computing environmental contribution `a_i·q_i,r`.
2. At each time step compute exactly one member/nutrient kinetic budget **`U_i,m=Vmax_i,m·S_m/(Km+S_m)`**. Enforce **`Σ_r max(−q_i,r,0) ≤ U_i,m`** across all that member's channels for the nutrient. A linear formulation may use nonnegative auxiliaries `u_r≥−q_i,r`, with `Σu_r≤U_i,m`, or an equivalent verified directional-variable formulation. For a singleton, the supplying raw bound follows the sign of `c_pool,r/a_i`: negative raw flux for positive coefficient, positive raw flux for negative coefficient. Keep legitimate secretion available and preserve all independent model constraints.
3. Do not give each duplicate the full cap, divide the budget equally among channels, discard channels, or reject otherwise legitimate duplicates. The constraint should permit the LP to allocate the single budget to whichever channel is feasible. A blocked duplicate must not consume a fixed share of the available budget.
4. Keep the environmental limit on its community-biomass basis and bound it by the abundance-weighted budgets once. Update constraints consistently after abundance changes. Derive any fallback Vmax in physical supplying coordinates rather than from `−lower_bound`; retain explicit reaction-flux semantics for ordinary `MediumSpec`.
5. Required real-LP regressions: the zero-cap orientation pair above; the duplicate pair above; mixed forward/reverse duplicates; a blocked duplicate with a usable alternative; unequal abundances and at least two integration steps. Assert **per-member** uptake against the budget and the environment balance, plus unchanged legitimate duplicate/static readout. Keep MICOM construction/tradeoff delegation, input nonunit rejection and source-GEM immutability.
