2. MAP_SPEC POLICY FIELD
========================
`uppercase_stereoisomer_suffix_folded` flipped true -> false in
cmig/core/host_map.py host_map_policy().

Done as a measurement rather than a literal:
    "uppercase_stereoisomer_suffix_folded": (
        _normalize_metabolite_id("lac__D_e") == _normalize_metabolite_id("lac__L_e")
    ),
    "case_folded": (
        _normalize_metabolite_id("AC_e") == _normalize_metabolite_id("ac_e")
    ),
Rationale: this field is hashed, and it went stale exactly once — it recorded
the CC-7 defect as though it were policy and kept recording it after the fix.
A restated policy can go stale; an asked one cannot. This matches the module's
own stated design philosophy ("a described policy cannot certify an artifact;
a measured one can"). Observed values now: folded=False, case_folded=True.

Also corrected a comment on HOST_MAP_NEEDS_REVIEW_TYPES that still claimed the
needs_review bucket includes "D/L stereoisomer swaps" — it cannot any more,
because the normalizer no longer pairs lac__D with lac__L.
