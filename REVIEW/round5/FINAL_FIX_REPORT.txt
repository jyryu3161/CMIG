9. JUDGEMENT CALLS, STATED SO THEY CAN BE OVERRULED
================================================================================
1. Item 1 keeps the member's ROW and drops only the unmeasured bar, instead of
   omitting the member entirely as abundance-impact does. Reason: a member's
   community leg often solves when its alone leg does not; omitting the row would
   discard a real measurement to hide a fabricated one. The count in the title is
   "members not evaluable", i.e. members with at least one unmeasured leg.
2. Item 3 sets `community_growth` to NaN only when the COMMUNITY solve failed, not
   whenever the row is unevaluable. An optimal community growth is a real number
   even when the host LP is infeasible, and blanking it would be the same class of
   error in the other direction.
3. `inspect-run` exits non-zero ONLY on an artifact mismatch, not on
   `status: failed`. Widening it would change the exit code of every inspection of
   a legitimately-failed run and is a separate decision; I kept the blast radius
   to the defect. Recorded in `_inspect_run_exit_code`'s docstring.
4. On a mismatch I overwrite the reported `status` rather than adding a parallel
   field only. The reviewer's instruction was explicit ("stop reporting `status: ok`
   over it") and a gate written against `status` is the documented consumer. The
   manifest's own status is not lost — it is in `manifest.status`, and a test pins
   its continued visibility.
5. I added the host-search figure annotation (§5) beyond the brief's letter,
   because excluding the failures made the figure silent rather than wrong, and the
   binding ruling covers silence.
6. I did NOT touch the reviewer's remaining P1/P2 findings that were out of scope:
   P1-c (`n_candidates_evaluated` counts cache entries), P1-d (gene-KO all-failed
   manifest tier), P1-e for the three commands other than sweep, P2-b/c
   (`community_growth = 0` for an arm that never ran, in host_ko_impact.csv and the
   multi-target `search_unevaluated.csv`), P2-d (dFBA figure carries no status).
   P2-b and P2-c are the same class as item 3 and are the obvious next candidates;
   they are one-line changes each but sit in writers with their own goldens, so I
